Our Mission

CEAN gives citizens a simple way to document environmental problems in their communities. Reports can include photographs, locations and descriptions so that environmental issues can be identified and addressed more efficiently.

What Can You Report?

Help identify environmental problems in your community.

🌫️

Air Pollution

Report smoke, open burning, dust clouds, industrial emissions and vehicle pollution.

💧

Water Pollution

Report contaminated water, illegal discharge, polluted rivers and lakes.

🗑️

Solid Waste

Report illegal dumping, overflowing bins, construction waste and littering.

🔊

Noise Pollution

Report excessive noise from construction, vehicles, generators and loudspeakers.
